Leg Scan Field Test

Leg Scan Field Test

Three photos and a bank card. The app finds the leg and foot on its own and measures ankle and calf circumference, foot length and foot width.

Photos are measured on this phone and never uploaded.

Before you start

Get set up

Plain wall and floor. Stand in front of a wall with no pattern. Bare leg, shorts or rolled-up pants.

Any bank, gift or ID card. Show the back, so no card number is in the photo.

A helper takes the photos. The person being measured stands still with weight on both feet.

Results
These results come from computer-drawn sample photos with known sizes, so they show how close the measuring is under ideal conditions.

Measurements

Circumference up the leg

Measured every centimeter from the front and side outlines and estimated as an oval at each height.

Compare with a tape measure

Measure the same spots with a tape and enter the values. Saved comparisons go to the test log.

Accuracy log
